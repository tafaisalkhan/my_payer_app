My Namaz Scheduler

Privacy Policy for Google Play Store

Package: com.mynamaz.scheduler Local-First Architecture Zero Tracking Effective: Oct 12, 2026
Key Privacy Commitment: My Namaz Scheduler is designed with strict privacy-by-design principles. We do not require accounts, we do not collect personal identifiers, and all calculations (prayer times, Qibla direction, task schedules) execute strictly offline on your device.

1. Overview and Core Principles

Welcome to My Namaz Scheduler ("we", "our", or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user privacy and device permissions. We believe your spiritual practices, schedule, and personal habits should remain completely private.

  • No User Account: No registration, login, email, phone number, or name is required.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics (like Firebase Analytics or Facebook SDK), or telemetry trackers.
  • Offline Storage: Prayer logs, work tasks, and Quran recitation bookmarks are stored in your device's private SQLite database.

2. Permissions and Data Handling

A. Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Purpose: To calculate precise Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and calculate the magnetic bearing towards the Holy Kaaba in Makkah (Qibla Compass).

Processing: Your coordinates are processed entirely in local device memory via standard astronomical solar algorithms. Your location is never uploaded, shared, or stored on remote servers.

B. Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM)

Purpose: Required by Android to sound the Adhan (call to prayer) and deliver critical Quran recitation and task timeout alerts at the exact scheduled second, even when your device is locked or in battery-saving/Doze mode.

Processing: Registered directly with Android's local AlarmManager.

C. Audio Focus & Media Controls

Purpose: The application requests standard Android audio focus during Adhan playback to temporarily pause background media players (e.g., YouTube, Spotify, podcasts) and automatically resumes playback when the Adhan concludes.

Volume Control: Optional volume boosting ensures prayer calls are audible, automatically restoring previous volume levels after playback finishes.

D. Compass & Sensors (Magnetometer)

Purpose: Real-time direction heading for the interactive Qibla Compass. Sensor readings are read in real-time in memory and discarded immediately.

3. Permissions Table

Permission Exact Purpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Calculate local solar prayer times and Qibla direction. Processed strictly on device.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Deliver Adhan, Quran, and work task schedule reminders.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM Trigger prayer call Adhan and alarms at the exact scheduled second.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Reliable background Adhan audio playback and media pause/resume.
WAKE_LOCK Wake the device screen during scheduled prayer call.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All application records (prayer logs, task sessions, settings) remain exclusively inside your device's private sandbox.

  • Manual Deletion: You can wipe all local logs and history at any time inside the app via Settings > Data & Privacy > Clear Local Data.
  • Uninstalling: Removing the app permanently purges all local databases and files from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

The application is intended for general audiences. Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not collect or solicit information from children under 13.
